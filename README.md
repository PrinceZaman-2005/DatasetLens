# DatasetLens 🔍

Open-source dataset inspection and quality analysis toolkit for ML datasets.

---

## Introduction

**Inspect your datasets before training models!**

DatasetLens helps you understand what is inside your datasets before they reach your training pipeline.

It can help identify:

* File formats
* Dataset composition
* Supported and unsupported files
* Broken or corrupt files
* Duplicate files
* Encoding and language information
* Dataset-level statistics

The goal is simple: **"Know your dataset before you train your model."**
